Yan Ding

Robotics researcher working on embodied AI.

I work on robot learning and mobile manipulation, connecting scalable data collection, vision-language-action models, task and motion planning, and real-world deployment. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the State University of New York at Binghamton. Previously, I was CTO at Lumos Robotics (until August 2026) and One Star Robotics, and a researcher at Shanghai AI Laboratory. FastUMI is one of my key efforts in connecting data collection, model training, and deployment for embodied manipulation.

Part-time Researcher, Fudan University Visiting Scholar, Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University Recruiting interns and prospective Master's / Ph.D. students.
